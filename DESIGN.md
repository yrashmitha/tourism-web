# Route Story

The established visual world is an editorial Sri Lanka travel atlas. This document records the approved world already implemented in the project; it does not approve a replacement hero composition.

- Map paper `#F5F3EC`, deep ink `#172D3B`, botanical green `#3E7566`, vermilion routes `#D24C36`, darker vermilion action `#B83F2E`.
- Noto Serif JP for large headings; Noto Sans JP for body and controls; Sofia for small Latin travel notes. Preserve the bilingual Japanese/English interface.
- Deliberate asymmetric imagery, cartographic route lines and markers, fine rules, generous space, legible text on clear surfaces. Photos may be full bleed or separately framed. Do not overlap paragraph text with busy scenery.
- Warm editorial sections rather than a repeated grid of identical cards. Journey duration is meaningful information, and itinerary days/planning stages may be numbered.
- WhatsApp is the main action. Route-specific messages keep enquiries relevant. No enquiry forms or checkout.
- Use semantic native disclosure controls for itinerary and service details. Keyboard focus must remain visible. Mobile layouts use a full readable column and must fit from 320 CSS pixels.
- Motion is restrained and purposeful: scene entrance, route wayfinding, disclosure feedback; respect reduced motion. Core content remains visible without client scripting.
- Existing custom images and generated road scenery are illustrative. Provenance lives beside image files; do not imply generated people, transport or accommodation are the client's real operations.

The homepage hero is under review after the user identified hard collage seams and unreadable scene labels. Three replacement compositions are being prepared under `.impeccable/hero-refresh-options.json`. Its palette and type remain Route Story.
