# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, deployed to Vercel as a static website. No application database for the agreed scope.

## Users

Japanese travelers are the primary audience. The website must support Japanese and English.

## Product Purpose

Showcase a client's travel and tour guide business and its tour packages. Help visitors choose a tour and contact the business through WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- WhatsApp is the sole enquiry and booking contact channel.
- No enquiry forms, online payments, or online reservation system.
- The experience must be fully responsive and designed mobile-first for Japanese visitors using phones.
- The client wants the page set represented by the Travel with Sampath reference: Home, Packages, Services, and Contact. This is a reference for page scope and content organization, not a source for copied claims or media.
- The client has supplied package and business details to the user through WhatsApp. Those details will be reviewed later and are not yet available in this project.
- Business name, contact number, destinations actually served, itineraries, prices, inclusions, and exact tour style are open decisions pending that review.
- Whether the operator provides Japanese-speaking guides or Japanese-language WhatsApp support is unconfirmed.
- Content maintenance workflow is not yet agreed.

## Brand Commitments

The business name and existing identity are not yet known. The user wants the website to feel premium, with animation and custom generated imagery. Agree the design, colors, and fonts with the user before implementing the website.

## Evidence on Hand

Reference sites supplied by the user: https://travelwithsampath.com/ and https://jetwingtravels.com/. These inform the business model and design discussion; their commercial claims, reviews, certifications, package details, and imagery are not evidence about the client.

No client logo, photographs, verified testimonials, or package document has been supplied to this project. Generated imagery must not be presented as evidence of the client's actual guides, guests, vehicles, or accommodation.

## Product Principles

- Give Japanese and English visitors clear tour information.
- Make it easy to start a relevant WhatsApp conversation.
- Use confirmed client facts for commercial claims.
- Make tour content and mobile navigation readable alongside the visual presentation.
